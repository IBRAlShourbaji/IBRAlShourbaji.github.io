Dr. Ibrahim Al-Shourbaji

About

Ibrahim AlShourbaji received the Ph.D. degree from the University of Hertfordshire, U.K. He is currently with Jazan University, Saudi Arabia. His research includes AI, deep learning, optimization, and biomedical signal analysis.

  • Feature Selection Systems using Metaheuristics
  • Deep Learning Engineering Applications

Research Output
