About

Dr. Ibrahim Al-Shourbaji received his Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence and Optimization from the University of Hertfordshire, U.K., and is currently an Assistant Professor at Jazan University, Saudi Arabia. His research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, metaheuristic optimization, and deep learning, with a focus on developing intelligent and efficient algorithms for complex real-world problems, particularly in healthcare and signal processing. He has published in leading international journals and actively contributes to the scientific community as a reviewer for top-tier publishers, including Elsevier, Springer, and IEEE.
